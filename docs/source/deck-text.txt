Overview of IWMS
The Integrated Waste Management Suite (IWMS) is a digital platform built to modernize, streamline, and optimize municipal solid waste 
management. It brings together IoT-enabled sensors, GPS-tracked vehicles, QR-based household verification, CCTV monitoring, and 
mobile/web platforms to ensure seamless operations.
ZIGMA - IWMS
The system ensures:

Transparency & Accountability – End-to-end traceability of waste movement.

Fraud Prevention – Eliminates manual loopholes with automated verification.

Automated Collection Verification – QR codes validate household and bin-level pickups.

Accurate Weighment Tracking – IoT load cells and weighbridge integration.

Real-time Citizen Engagement – Notifications, feedback collection, and grievance handling via app/portal.

Regulatory Compliance – Aligned with Solid Waste Management (SWM) Rules for sustainable urban operations.

Sustainability – Promotes segregation, recycling, composting, and eco-friendly disposal.

Processing – Scientific segregation and treatment of wet, dry, hazardous, sanitary, and glass waste.

Disposal & Rejects – Safe disposal of non-combustible, non-compostable, non-reusable, non-recyclable rejects at engineered landfills
.

Landfill Management – Tracks inflow, balance life, fire safety, informal activity monitoring, compliance.

IWMS Model 
The system integrates web and app-based monitoring tools with an IoT-enabled navigation system to ensure efficient waste collection. It 
uses QR code-based tracking for accountability, connects with the Integrated Command and Control Centre (ICCC) for centralized oversight, 
and includes a grievance redressal system to address citizen concerns effectively. All operations will strictly follow the Solid Waste 
Management (SWM) Rules to ensure compliance with regulatory protocols.

Phased Implementation (12-Month Roadmap)
ZIGMA - IWMS
Phase 1 (0–1 month): Weighbridge Monitoring System(accurate inflow measurement, integrated with landfill and 
processing data)
Phase 2 (Month 1–2): Processing System Setup(facility mapping, segregation of organic, dry, and mixed waste)
Phase 3 (Month 2–3): Landfill Management Module(inflow tracking, analytics dashboards, landfill capacity, fire & safety, 
compliance)
Phase 4 (Month 3–6):
GPS Tracking & Route Optimization
QR Code Rollout for households, apartments, bulk waste generators, and bins
Phase 5 (Month 6–9): User Charge (integrated billing, online/offline payment options)
Phase 6 (Month 9–12): Grievance Redressal Mechanism(citizen app/portal, ticketing, escalation matrix) .
Outcome: Full IWMS operational in 12 months, with scalability for faster rollout if manpower is available.
